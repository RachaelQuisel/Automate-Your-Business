## Part three: the may-agree list and the never-agree list

### The caller may agree to

Tick each one that applies to this call. Cross out any one that does not.

- [ ] Confirm a time or a delivery window that is already on the calendar.
- [ ] Ask for a reference number, a name, and a callback number.
- [ ] Ask for a document to be sent to this email address: ______________________________
- [ ] ______________________________ (your own line)

### The caller must never agree to

These are the limits. If the person on the other end offers one of these, the caller writes it down exactly and brings it back. The caller does not accept it on the call.

- A new price, a discount, or any change to what something costs.
- A changed date or time that moves money, a crew, or a guest or customer.
- A new service, a contract change, or a renewal.
- A payment, a deposit, or giving card or bank details.

## Part five: file the outcome, with the checklist

A person reads each summary before it counts as done. Until that reading happens, the call is not finished.

The person who reads checks each item and ticks it when it is true:

- [ ] The facts in the summary match what the transcript or notes actually say.
- [ ] Every promise made on the call is written down, with its date.
- [ ] Nothing on the never-agree list was agreed to.
- [ ] Every fact a decision depends on is named with its source (the person spoken to, the reference number, the time).
- [ ] Anything unclear has a named owner and a follow-up date.
- [ ] The summary is filed where the next person will find it before the next call.
