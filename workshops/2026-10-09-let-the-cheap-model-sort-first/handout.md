### The three ideas (tape this to your monitor)

1. **Decide first, act second.** The cheap decider sees everything. The expensive doer sees only what matters.
2. **A decision is a choice, not an essay.** Picks and scores: duplicate or not, how sure, or "unsure." No paragraphs.
3. **Watch what it does not do.** The savings are the calls you never paid for. Count the silence.

### Homework (one week, deadline October 10th)

Write one decision rule and score a real pile this week, before the free window closes. Test the decider on your actual mess, count the expensive calls it avoided, and name the owner of the unsure queue. Bring back: your decision rule in one breath, what the real-pile test caught, and how many expensive calls you avoided. Show the page to one person who knows your business and ask what you left out.
