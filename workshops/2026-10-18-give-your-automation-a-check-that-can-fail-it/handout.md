### The three ideas (tape this to your monitor)

1. **Check the work, not the story about the work.** A maker's summary is not evidence.
2. **Averages negotiate. Gates do not.** One never-failure stops the line.
3. **A check you have never seen fail is a decoration.** Rehearse the failure in private.

### The numbers that make the case

From this week's stories: a self-checking video system passed more than 20 checks for 4 days while 4 published videos each rewound 39 times at scene changes. An evaluated agent scored 90% across 20 tasks and still got a HOLD, because 1 task broke an authority rule. A film agent cut every number that had no receipt in its facts file, and drove flagged frozen frames from 10 in the first cut to 0 in the final.

### Homework (within two weeks, three answers)

Run your rehearsal. Bring back: did it stop · did the alert name the failure plainly · how long until the named human saw it. If it waved the bad work through, bring that result instead. It is the most valuable page in the room.
