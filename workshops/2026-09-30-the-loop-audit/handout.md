# The Loop Audit

**Automate Your Business** · Print one per person, double-sided.

Use this to map one piece of recurring work in your business as a complete loop. You are not
building it today. You are naming it.

---

## Page 1 — The five parts

A loop that runs without you has all five. Most people skip the fifth.

**1. Trigger** — the one event that starts it. Not a person remembering.

**2. Steps** — what happens, in order, every single time.

**3. Decision** — the one fork in the middle. Usually one question, two answers.

**4. Output** — the thing that exists when it finishes.

**5. Escape hatch** — what happens when it gets stuck, and who it calls.

### Step C — The check

- Is your trigger an event, not a person remembering? ☐

Any box unchecked is where the loop will break first.

## If you only do one thing

Write this sentence and do it this week:

> This week I will build the trigger for my ____________________ loop.

Just the trigger. Not the steps. When something in your business can start itself, you have
proven the whole thing works — and the steps are the easy part.
