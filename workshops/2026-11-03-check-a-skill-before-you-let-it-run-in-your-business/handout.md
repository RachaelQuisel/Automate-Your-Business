# Check a Skill Before You Let It Run in Your Business

Teach date: Friday, March 12, 2027. Print this handout. Fill it in by hand or on a computer during the workshop. Everything on it is yours to keep: it becomes your business's approval record for this skill, and the template for every skill after it.

## Part one: the skill and the job it claims

- The skill's name, exactly as written by its author: ______________________________
- Where you found it (the full web address): ______________________________
- Its job in one sentence, in its author's words: ______________________________
- The job you actually want it to do in your business: ______________________________

## Part two: the five-minute read

Read the instructions file start to finish, and every script with it.

- Anything that sends data outside your business? Write what, and where it goes: ______________________________
- Anything that reads folders or accounts the job does not need? Write what: ______________________________
- Anything that changes your helper's own settings or its other skills? Write what: ______________________________
- Does the install command name the exact project you read, the same owner and the same name (yes or no)? ______________________________
- What leaves your machine when this skill runs, including any sign-in or hosted service it asks for: ______________________________
- Does the access match the job sentence? Where is the access bigger than the job: ______________________________

## Part three: scripts get the slow read

- Does this skill contain scripts, small programs that run (yes or no)? ______________________________
- If yes, how many script files, and the one you read most slowly: ______________________________
- In that script, the line or step you least understand, in your own words: ______________________________
- Remember: in the study behind the scanner, skills with scripts were more than two times as likely to contain a flaw as skills that are only instructions.

## Part four: the scan, before you download

Scan the skill from its web address first, so the check happens before anything touches your computer.

- Date and time of the scan: ______________________________
- The score, from 0 to 100: ______________________________
- The verdict (safe, caution, or do not install). The do-not-install line is 51: ______________________________
- The most serious finding, in plain words: ______________________________
- Anything the scanner found that your read missed: ______________________________
- Anything your read found that the scanner did not flag: ______________________________
- Where the full scan report is saved: ______________________________

## Part five: the placement decision

- Decision (install into one project first, install everywhere, or do not install): ______________________________
- If one project first: which project, and what would have to happen before it goes wider: ______________________________
- The exact version pinned, so it cannot change quietly: ______________________________
- The smallest access this skill needs for its job, and the access you actually gave it: ______________________________
- The date you will review this skill again: ______________________________

## Part six: the approval record

- Read by (name): ______________________________
- Scanned by (name): ______________________________
- Approved by (name): ______________________________
- Date of approval: ______________________________
- One sentence: why this skill is trusted in this business: ______________________________

## One-sentence finish

Read this out loud at the end of the workshop:

"The skill ____________________ was read by ____________________, scanned with a score of ____________________, and is installed in ____________________ at version ____________________, approved by ____________________ on ____________________."

Assignment: this week, run the same check on every skill already installed in your business, starting with any skill that contains scripts. Scan each one, write its score and version on the approval page, and remove any skill nobody can explain the job of. Bring the completed page, and the one finding that surprised you most, to your next session.
