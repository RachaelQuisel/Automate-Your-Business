### Filled example: lead intake for a vacation rental

**Workflow:** 1: lead intake and qualification
**Start signal:** when a new inquiry email arrives in my inbox
**AI decides:** whether the guest's dates are open and the group fits the house rules, and scores the fit 1 to 5
**Automatic:** log the inquiry on the calendar, score the fit, draft a reply in my voice
**Human gate:** I read the draft, edit it if I want, and press send myself. Nothing reaches a guest without me.
**Failure looks like:** a bad-fit guest gets a 5, or a draft sits in my inbox unsent and the guest books elsewhere
**Measure:** how many inquiries got a personal reply within one hour
