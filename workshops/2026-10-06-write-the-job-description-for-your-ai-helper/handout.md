### The three ideas (tape this to your monitor)

1. **Describe the job, don't draw the wiring.** The agent figures out the steps. Your work is the description.
2. **The plan is the thing you audit.** Check what it decided to do, not just what it did.
3. **The prenup goes on the back.** Write the exit terms before you embed. Removing an embedded helper is painful.

### Homework (two weeks)

Run your helper from this page. Bring back: what the description got right, what the plan review caught, and one line you'd add to the prenup. Show the page to one person who knows your business and ask what you left out.
