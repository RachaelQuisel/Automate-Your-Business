# Let Your AI Helper Work in the Background, and Ask Only When It Needs You

Teach date: TBD. Print this handout. Fill it in by hand or on a computer during the workshop. Everything on it is yours to keep: it is the rule card for one background job, the page you hand to whoever installs the hub, and the template for every unattended job after this one.

## Part seven: the install check (for whoever installs the hub)

## One-sentence finish

Read this out loud at the end of the workshop:

"My helper ____________________ in the ____________________ folder at ____________________. It may ____________________ on its own. It asks ____________________ before ____________________. It stops when ____________________, and ____________________ shows me why."

Assignment: this week, install the hub on one computer that does not hold your passwords, or have your technical person do it, and run the trial. Then run your rule card as a real rule for five mornings with the permission left at edit, nothing that sends, spends, or deletes signed in where the helper can reach it, and the cards it sends on your ask line coming to you. Each morning, read the inputs yourself and check that every ask-line item produced a card, because the ask line is an instruction the helper may not follow. Write down what it cost per day from the status line, the one question it asked that you did not expect, and any card it should have sent but did not. Bring the card and those three notes to your next session.

**If you only do one thing:** write the lock half of line four. List what the helper must not be able to reach, then make sure nothing on that list is signed in where it runs.
