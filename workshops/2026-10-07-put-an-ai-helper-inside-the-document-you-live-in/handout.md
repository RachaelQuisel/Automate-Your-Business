### The three ideas (tape this to your monitor)

1. **Work where the work is.** The agent comes to your document. Stop commuting with copy-paste.
2. **Ask before it edits.** The review is the job. The fortieth proposed edit deserves the same attention as the first.
3. **The prenup goes on the back.** Write the exit terms before you embed. Removing an embedded helper is painful.

### Homework (two weeks)

Run the helper in your document. Bring back: what the review caught, what the fortieth edit felt like, and one line you'd add to the prenup. Show the page to one person who knows your business and ask what you left out.
