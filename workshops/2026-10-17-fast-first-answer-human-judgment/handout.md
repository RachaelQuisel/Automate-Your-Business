### The three ideas (tape this to your monitor)

1. **Book it on the first contact.** A promise to call back is where good leads leave.
2. **Hybrid beats either alone.** Machine for speed, human for judgment.
3. **Name the handoff line.** Write where the AI stops before you automate anything.

### The numbers that make the case

From three point two million law firm calls (Smith.ai, October 16, 2026): human only booked 59 of 100 qualified leads, hybrid AI and human booked 52 at less than half the cost, AI only booked 33. Firms offering instant booking converted 64 of 100. Firms without it converted 12, and 7 after hours.

### Homework (two weeks, four numbers)

Each week count: first contacts · screened with your list · offered instant booking · booked. Bring back the four numbers and your handoff line. If booking did not move, bring the leak you found instead.
