### Set-up checklist

- [ ] Instructions written in plain words (3 sentences is fine)
- [ ] Start signal set — a specific time, or a specific event
- [ ] Ran it once and looked at what came out
- [ ] Named the person who'll notice if it ever *stops* running
- [ ] Re-read date on my calendar

### Three honest warnings

1. **Instructions go stale.** Re-read yours once a month, or the computer keeps doing last month's job with total confidence.
2. **Nobody's watching.** Pick someone who'll notice if it goes quiet. Jobs that run silently fail silently.
3. **The computer assembles — you decide.** Never hand it the apology, the refund, or anything that needs judgment.
