## One-sentence finish

Read this out loud at the end of the workshop:

"Our nine jobs are ____________________. Good for each one is written in ____________________. Correctness is scored by ____________________, taste is scored by me in words, and the jobs no agent gets yet are ____________________ until we retest on ____________________."

Assignment: this week, run your nine jobs on the one agent you use now, even if you test no other. Score correctness from the checklist with a scorer who did not do the work, write your taste words the same day, and move any job both the checklist and your judgment failed onto the list of jobs no agent gets yet. Bring one failed job, and the checklist line it failed, to your next session.
