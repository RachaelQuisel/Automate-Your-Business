## Part four: the spending limit and the handoff

- Weekly limit for this job, in dollars: ______________
- Where I will check the actual spend, and on which day each week: ______________________________
- When the limit is reached, the job will (pause, hand back, or other, say which): ______________________________
- The unfinished work goes to this named person, the same day: ______________________________
- This person is told the stop happened, by this method they actually read: ______________________________
- The highest-cost surprise I found when I looked at last month's spend, and its explanation: ______________________________

## Part five: the failure rule, in four lines

Copy the shape, then write yours in your own words and post it where the whole team can find it.

1. When a test fails twice in a row, the agent stops.
2. This named person is told the same day: ______________________________
3. This named person, and only this person, can restart it: ______________________________
4. Before it restarts, the failure is fixed and all five tests pass again.

Where the signed rule lives (a place the agent cannot edit): ______________________________

## Part six: the rehearsal

Pull the agent offline once today, on a quiet job, while nothing is wrong.

- Time the stop took, from decision to fully stopped: ______________
- Who noticed, and how: ______________________________
- Did the handoff happen the way Part four says? If not, what will you change: ______________________________
- Restart completed through the full rule, all five tests passing (date and initials): ______________

Where the tests, right answers, and scores are kept (a place you own, that the agent cannot edit): ______________________________

Beside each recorded run, note the platform, the model, and the mode, plus any announced retirement date for any of them: ______________________________

## One-sentence finish

Read this out loud at the end of the workshop:

"My job ____________________ now has five tests with right answers in my own words, a weekly limit of ____________________ dollars with the handoff going to ____________________, and the one person who can stop it is ____________________, who has already done it once today."

Assignment: this week, run your five tests on every trusted agent job you have, on the same day, and write the scores where a second person can see them. Add one new test for every real failure you find. Bring your inventory, your scores, and the story of your rehearsal, how long the stop took and who noticed, to your next session.
