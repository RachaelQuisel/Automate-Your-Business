### The three ideas (tape this to your monitor)

1. **Every helper gets an authority card.** Three columns: may do alone, needs my tap, never touches.
2. **Authority is a ladder, not a switch.** Start on rung one. Boring is the promotion criterion.
3. **The card is the audit.** Write the card before it runs. The receipts prove what it did after.

### Homework (two weeks)

Run your helper on the rung you assigned. Bring back: what it did alone, what it brought you for a tap, and one item you would move to a different column. If the two weeks were boring, promote it one rung.
