# Turn Your Best Tricks Into Skills Your Whole Team Can Use

## Part six: run it with a second person

Have someone who is not the owner run the skill once during the workshop, on real material.

- Who ran it: ______________________________
- Every place they had to guess, in their words: ______________________________
- The fix you will make to the skill for each guess: ______________________________
- Would the result have been safe to receive if you were the customer? If not, what changes before anyone runs it again: ______________________________

## One-sentence finish

Read this out loud at the end of the workshop:

"My job ____________________ lived with ____________________, and now it is a written skill owned by ____________________, living in ____________________, with its checkpoint at ____________________."

Assignment: this week, run your new skill three times with a second person, not its owner, and write down every place they had to guess. Fix the guesses in the skill, with a new date. Then write one more skill for the next trick that lives in one person's head. Bring both skills, and the list of guesses, to your next session.
