## Part one: the morning brief

The brief arrives at (time): ______________  On this screen (the one you open first): ______________

The five slots. Write what belongs in each slot for your business.

1. What came in overnight that needs me: ______________________________
2. Today's money in and out: ______________________________
3. The one job that is stuck: ______________________________
4. The one person waiting on me: ______________________________

Rule: five slots, no more. A brief with twenty items is a second inbox.

## The opening rule

The bargain, in full sentences:

- The helper brings the brief at the time above, knocks only for the events above, and waits at the four gates above.
- I open the brief every morning. Person who opens it (name): ______________________________
- If the brief is not opened for three mornings in a row, we change the time, the screen, or the slots. We do not blame the helper first.

## The three currencies check

A speaking-up page spends three things. Price all three.

- Money: the helper subscription this page governs costs $ ______________ per month, and it earns that money only if the brief is opened and the knocks are acted on.
- Attention: knocks allowed per day before the knock list gets rewritten: ______________ knocks. Brief opening time each morning: ______________ minutes.
- Trust: the first time the helper acts at a gate without approval, it loses that gate for a month and every action of that kind waits for a person. Person who enforces this (name): ______________________________

## One-sentence finish

Read this out loud at the end of the workshop:

"My helper brings me ____________________ every morning at __________. It may knock only when ______________________________. It waits for ____________________ before it sends, pays, signs, or deletes anything. It may never ______________________________."
