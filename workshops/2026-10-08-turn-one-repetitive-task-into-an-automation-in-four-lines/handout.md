### The three ideas (tape this to your monitor)

1. **Describe it, don't wire it.** Trigger, source, transform, destination. Four lines replace the plumbing.
2. **Test on real data before you publish.** The demo is not the deployment. The test is the job.
3. **Boring is the promotion criterion.** If the first run was boring, the description was good.

### Homework (one week, deadline October 10th)

Build one automation before the free decision-model window closes. Four lines, built in the chat panel, tested on your real data, three production rules checked. Bring back: your four-line prompt, what the real-data test caught, and what the first boring run felt like. Show the page to one person who knows your business and ask what you left out.
