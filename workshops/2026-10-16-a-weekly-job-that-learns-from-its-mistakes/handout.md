### The three ideas (tape this to your monitor)

1. **Plan, work, review, compound.** Four steps, in that order, every time.
2. **One lesson, filed where the next run finds it.** Short, specific, dated.
3. **Silence is not approval.** Name the reviewer, and define a pass.

### Homework (four runs, one month)

Run the loop four times on the same job. Ten minutes of review each time, exactly one lesson filed where the next run starts. Bring back: your plan page, your four lessons, and the first trap the loop caught before it cost you money. Show the page to one person who knows your business and ask what you left out.
