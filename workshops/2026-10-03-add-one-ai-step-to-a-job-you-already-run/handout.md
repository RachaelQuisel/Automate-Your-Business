### The three ideas (tape this to your monitor)

1. **The agent is a step, not the system.** One job inside a workflow you already understand.
2. **The leash is a checkbox.** Reading is free; sending, publishing, and spending ask first.
3. **Boring beats clever.** New situations get AI; repeated steps stay automatic and cheap. Watch it for a month.
