# Series of Loops: post ideas (draft)

This folder holds draft LinkedIn and Instagram post ideas. They come from one podcast episode: Lenny's Podcast, "Why companies are becoming a series of loops," with Anish Acharya.

This is a draft in progress. Content Maker handed it off on Oct 7, 2026. Nothing here has been posted. Items marked **[confirm]** still need Rachael's check.

## What's here

- `snipd-ideas.md`: the post ideas, captions, and short video outlines.
- `snipd-sources.md`: where the Snipd notes live and what could be read on Oct 7, 2026.
- `visuals/`: a 6-slide carousel as PNGs (`loop-carousel-01.png` to `loop-carousel-06.png`) and as one PDF (`loop-carousel.pdf`).
- `visuals/src/build.py`: the script that built the carousel.

## Sources

The verbatim snips are already in this repo at `sources/snips/2026-09-22-series-of-loops.md`. They are not copied here.
