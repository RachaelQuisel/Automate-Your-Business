## 1. INSIGHT — Salesforce's Four-Part Acquisition Test
- Salesforce evaluates acquisitions through strategic fit, acceleration potential, cultural alignment, and shareholder value.
- The hardest test is valuation and the timeline for value accretion when attractive assets are expensive.

## 2. INSIGHT — AI Is Moving Software Beyond Seat Pricing
- AI is pushing enterprise software toward consumption and outcome-based pricing instead of seat-based licenses.
- Salesforce increasingly charges around usage or resolved work, provided customers clearly recognize the value delivered.

## 3. ANECDOTE — Finn Proved Its Value In Two Weeks
- Salesforce deployed Finn internally in 14 days to test its rapid time-to-value.
- Finn now handles over 40% of help.salesforce traffic and resolves 65% without human escalation.

## 4. INSIGHT — Distribution Can Matter More Than Technology
- Salesforce distribution can compress years of startup sales-building into immediate global access to 15,000 reps.
- Founders must weigh that acceleration against continuing to raise capital, dilute ownership, and build distribution alone.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/45f4be18-eef1-4031-8cc5-cf68a57bacdd

## 5. ANECDOTE — Why Salesforce Buys Scarce AI Expertise
- Salesforce compares building with buying by examining its three-year roadmap, internal expertise, hiring difficulty, and integration speed.
- An acquisition can win when scarce AI talent and a six-to-nine-month process still beat internal development.

## 8. ADVICE — Choose Valuations With Exits In Mind
- Founders should evaluate valuations against realistic exit opportunities because very few startups reach public markets.
- Above $10 billion, the buyer universe becomes extremely small, making unsustainable valuations dangerous for companies and employees.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/b25c5dc2-ddc0-4f2a-9781-e3dd80f4ad13

## 9. ADVICE — Make Security A Deal-Critical Priority
- Prepare organized financials, customer contracts, retention data, privacy controls, and security before acquisition diligence.
- Red-team the technology first; Salesforce once reached a target's source code, employee data, and CEO laptop during penetration testing.
