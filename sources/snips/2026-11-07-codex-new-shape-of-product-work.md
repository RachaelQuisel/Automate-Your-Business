## 1. ADVICE — Pick Document Or Prototype Based On Purpose
- Pick the medium (doc vs prototype) based on the point you need to make, not on habit or tooling.
- Use documents for product clarity and prototypes to stress-test interactions, because prototypes can falsely signal production-readiness.

## 3. INSIGHT — Design Is Harder To Automate Than Code
- AI lags at design because grading design is harder and cultural/novelty factors matter more than correctness.
- Deeper challenges include mapping visual design to engineering abstractions and maintaining semantics across components.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/8f1f0ac3-036d-4386-9683-b27db30fc555

## 5. INSIGHT — Roles Won't Vanish But Gatekeeping Is Eroding
- Andrew warns against abolishing roles because specialties hold knowable best practices and depth matters.
- Tools-based gatekeeping is fading: switching roles and learning practices is easier now, so mindset beats syntax mastery.

## 6. ADVICE — Use Zone Defense For Product Coverage
- Use a zone defense model: spread product-minded people to cover the product landscape and avoid overlapping ownership.
- Hire engineers with product sense so features don't require constant review for coherence.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/1a9ca4cd-2418-46ad-b6db-4da47ad4eeec
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** Roles Shift, Depth Still Matters

## 7. ADVICE — Plan Broadly, Execute Near Term Precisely
- Keep long-range plans hazy and add detail only for near-term work; avoid false precision.
- Prototype many candidate features, shelve the ones waiting for model improvements, and revisit as models advance.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/c0290e23-09de-4249-812c-0f031e29b752

- Andrew describes trade-offs: in-app browser latency, enterprise login, key mappings and muscle memory across tools.
