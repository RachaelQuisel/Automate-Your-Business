## 1. ADVICE — Use AI to Find the Right Enterprise Contact
- Ask OpenAI or Anthropic to identify an executive and N-1 contact, then verify the names yourself.
- The outreach must feel different and promise executive-level alpha, or avoid a top-down enterprise strategy.

## 2. ADVICE — Make Enterprise Pitches Boardroom-Worthy
- Enterprise messaging must show how the product changes a business model or operating structure, not merely deliver incremental efficiency.
- Ask what the customer could tell their COO, CEO, or board the product enables them to do.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/cd311651-638f-44d6-a0d5-0ce85950f850
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** Sell What You Built

## 3. ADVICE — Enterprise Pitches Must Show Needle-Moving Impact
- Enterprise buyers respond to differentiated business models and AI-era ways of restructuring teams, not generic promises of efficiency.
- Test the pitch by asking what the buyer could tell their CEO or board the product enables.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/a376f109-7fce-4fa5-85bc-d2ba1de7d8f3
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** Sell What You Built

## 4. ADVICE — Run An Informal 30-Minute Intro Call And Let Them Talk
- Make the intro call informal, 30 minutes, one-on-one, and avoid demos or slides.
- Let them go first, extract their priorities, then tailor a bespoke frame based on what they reveal.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/abdd969f-5bb3-49bd-97d5-9c1ed009e7b2
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** The Discovery Call

## 5. ADVICE — Use Questions To Expose Enterprise Priorities
- Ask what must change, rather than anchoring prospects toward your product, so they reveal priorities and organizational pressure.
- Jen probes a law leader's plan to bring work in-house and asks how success would be measured.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/1d03c34e-d618-4930-8a08-75542c66cc44
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** The Discovery Call

## 6. ADVICE — Don't Record Intro Calls And Improvise Your Pitch
- Do not record intro calls; it makes buyers less open.
- Be off-the-record, dig deeper with probing follow-ups, then craft a customized pitch rather than a script.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/8e132d60-9460-4d0a-a7fc-942c85207874
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** The Discovery Call

## 7. ADVICE — Make The Demo A Carefully Earned Carrot
- Treat the demo as the carrot after the intro call, not an automatic next step.
- Bring the right stakeholders so you retain leverage to organize and rally the buying group.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/c06f8c6e-c080-4bd2-8918-115f24b1bbf2
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** The Demo

## 8. ADVICE — Co-Design The Demo With Your Champion
- Before the demo, hold a 15-minute prep call to identify the right attendees and tailor the product walkthrough.
- Ask the champion to pose questions that will resonate with the internal team, giving them fingerprints on the deal.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/1f906010-9129-421f-9b62-018cd2df8dcb
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** The Demo

## 9. ADVICE — Run A Pre-Demo To Co-Create The Group Demo
- Always run a 15–30 minute pre-demo with your champion to map the demo storyline and attendee list.
- Co-author which 2–3 product areas to show so the group demo feels tailored and the champion has ownership.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/d5cbd03a-a406-4175-81ec-afb4d032f721
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** The Demo

## 10. ADVICE — Never Demo Without Competitive Intel
- Jen Abel warns that a blind group demo makes you either a checkbox in due diligence or vulnerable to competitors.
- Build internal champions and gather intel through pre-demos before presenting to the larger buying group.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/2caa6d29-2f2f-4b3a-bcfe-2356cb21ada6

## 11. ADVICE — Make Every Demo Stakeholder Feel Prepared
- Identify everyone involved in the enterprise decision and learn what each person cares about before the demo.
- Your internal champion can reveal what to emphasize, avoid, and define as a successful demo.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/0c75fc28-c8b6-4ff1-84d8-b82cf029ebb3

## 12. INSIGHT — Demo The 20 Percent That Delivers 80 Percent Of Value
- Demo only the 20% of features that produce 80% of the value for that audience.
- Over-demoing dilutes the tailored narrative you built and lets buyers dismiss the product as misaligned.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/fba7703d-a874-446c-a12b-e5ac9fed4eb3
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** The Demo

## 13. ADVICE — Debrief Immediately Post Demo To Capture Raw Reactions
- Debrief immediately after the demo with your champion by phone or text to capture raw reactions.
- Ask where you won/lost and who could still kill the deal so you can proactively protect momentum.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/ee3bb1b9-1703-4ceb-b1bc-6fbc8bb4a06d
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** The Demo

## 14. ADVICE — Use A Two-To-Three Day Pilot Or Charge For Longer Integrations
- Offer a tightly time-boxed 48–72 hour pilot for demoable value and avoid charging for short pilots.
- If integration is required, charge for a month+ pilot and credit the fee on contract signature.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/819c5636-600d-4386-9efa-0e72917149fa
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** The Paid Pilot

## 15. ADVICE — Short Pilots Can Cut Enterprise Sales Cycles
- Time-box a simple pilot to two or three days so users quickly experience the product's value and you retain control of the sales cycle.
- For technical integrations, charge for a one- or two-month pilot and credit the fee toward the contract if they proceed.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/9e290080-426d-427b-9ccd-4fd7227e30f9
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** The Paid Pilot

## 16. ADVICE — Co-Author Specific Success Criteria For A Tight Pilot
- Co-author pilot success criteria and specific tasks for 3–4 power users to follow during the 48–72 hour test.
- Timebox activities tightly and define measurements so users don't meander and momentum stays controlled.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/2e0d4517-a58f-4b68-8c28-f013dab1f7dc
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** The Paid Pilot

## 17. ADVICE — Discuss Pricing Post-Demo And Make Champions Defend The ROI
- Delay formal pricing until post-demo or post-pilot; give only a ballpark earlier and work with your champion to craft the internal ROI defense.
- Never prematurely negotiate discounts; instead ask how they would make the case internally.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/a36d78f3-306c-4b4c-b17b-b51214243d38
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** Price It

## 18. INSIGHT — Expect A 25 To 35 Percent Win Rate From Qualified Leads
- Healthy enterprise win rates from qualified opportunities are roughly 25–35%.
- If your win rate is higher, you're likely pricing too low and leaving market value on the table.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/d4c5fe86-fbd8-497c-9361-bb7e42c93e41

- Create a deadline incentive and document the agreed procurement path the champion can forward internally.
