- Tulsee Doshi compares development to Snape's potions class: researchers control ingredients, but surprising "magic" emerges downstream.

- Its portfolio targets varied cost-quality points, from search users to engineers and cyber defenders.

- PMs translate desired behavior into specifications, rubrics, evaluations, and reward-modeling data.

## 5. INSIGHT — AI Makes Product Taste More Valuable

- Taste, insight, and consensus-building become more valuable as implementation speed accelerates.

## 6. ADVICE — Hire PMs Who Understand Model Vibes
- Hire PMs who use models daily, form strong opinions about their behavior, inspect data, build evaluations, and adapt quickly.
- In a field changing every two weeks, PMs must repeatedly challenge old roadmaps instead of treating plans as fixed.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/0d67d231-fcd6-4140-985c-33290934cbee

## 7. ADVICE — Start Your First Eval With Twenty Prompts
- Start evaluations with five to twenty prompts that stress-test a capability and compare models for visible signal.
- Rate responses yourself first, especially multi-turn trajectories, to clarify what "good" means before scaling evaluation.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/fc78141a-e331-4ace-9659-7b61b6834967

## 8. ANECDOTE — A Better Skin Tone Eval Changed Model Outcomes
- Google replaced the six-tone Fitzpatrick scale after recognizing it poorly represented global skin tones and served a different healthcare purpose.
- The ten-tone Monk scale improved evaluation and informed Pixel camera and image-generation work.

## 9. INSIGHT — Evaluate The Whole Production Experience
- Evaluating a model requires evaluating the entire user experience, including judges, filters, serving, and modality-specific qualities.
- For audio, naturalness, tone, and inflection can change materially after serving even when the underlying model performs well.

## 10. ADVICE — Use The 60-20-20 Rule For AI Products
- Allocate roughly 60% of product functionality to what works now, 20% to capabilities nearly ready, and 20% to promising glimpses.
- Keep the product adaptable so improving models can replace rigid workarounds rather than being forced into yesterday's structure.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/93c7b7ff-b628-43da-b14f-ae8d47938231

## 11. ADVICE — Give PMs Time To Build AI Skills
- Give teams dedicated time to experiment, share reusable technical skills, and run workshops that build confidence with AI tools.
- Tulsee's team plans a two-day workshop because constant execution leaves too little space to learn and create leverage.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/11ac5fb2-7e16-49c4-b307-e9eb25f816de

## 12. ADVICE — Ask If AI Can Do The Task First
- Insert the question "Can AI do this for me?" before starting recurring work, then test one concrete workflow.
- A single successful example can shift habits and build confidence to delegate increasingly varied tasks.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/7a9ed2e5-66c2-46cf-9fc3-ea715ac9da03

## 13. INSIGHT — Let AI Provide Data And Humans Provide Insight
- AI-generated documents help most when they gather facts, metrics, and feedback across sources; humans should supply judgment and insight.
- Separating data collection from interpretation reduces AI slop while preserving the productivity benefit.
