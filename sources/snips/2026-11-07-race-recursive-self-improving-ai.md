## 1. INSIGHT — When AI Improvement Becomes Truly Recursive
- True recursive self-improvement requires the system being improved to also perform the improving.
- Human-directed AI development remains autocatalysis because human brains still supply the ideas and optimization.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/d62fff94-37c6-416f-bbc8-01cd962655d4

## 2. INSIGHT — A Closed Loop Does Not Guarantee Takeoff
- Closing the loop can increase the rate of capability improvement, but it does not automatically produce exponential takeoff.
- Takeoff depends on whether improvement scales with capability or with a higher power of capability.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/aa1b86d9-5442-4ad4-905e-363d3750edfc
**Used in:** — · **Theme:** A Closed Loop Isn't Automatic Progress

## 3. INSIGHT — RSI Needs Experiments More Than Elegant Equations
- Researchers cannot infer RSI's trajectory from a feedback diagram alone; they must measure the system's empirical exponent.
- Even deep learning lacks a complete theory, so scaling laws remain primarily empirical guides.

## 9. INSIGHT — Inference Improvement Compounds On Itself
- Inference is an attractive RSI target because faster inference improves both model performance and the amount of experimentation possible.
- Compounding efficiency can be powerful while still eventually asymptoting rather than causing takeoff.

## 10. ADVICE — Choose Metrics That Can Reveal Real Takeoff
- Choose metrics that remain informative near their ceiling; benchmark accuracy cannot show exponential gains after approaching 100%.
- Replicate surprising results and reject cherry-picked improvements that collapse into noise.
