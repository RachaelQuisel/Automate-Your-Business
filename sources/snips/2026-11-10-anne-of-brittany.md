## 1. INSIGHT — Why Brittany Fought To Preserve Its Independence
- Brittany's autonomy was difficult to preserve because powerful neighbors repeatedly challenged its independence.
- France's postwar centralization efforts turned regional nobles and duchies into targets of royal sovereignty.

## 4. INSIGHT — Anne Turned Marriage Into Brittany's Shield
- Anne treated marriage as a diplomatic instrument for protecting Brittany rather than simply a personal arrangement.

## 5. ANECDOTE — Anne's Proxy Wedding Triggered A French Siege
- Anne secretly married Maximilian I by proxy, hoping the alliance would protect Brittany from France.
- France responded with war, and after Rennes was besieged, Anne accepted marriage to Charles VIII to end the conflict.
