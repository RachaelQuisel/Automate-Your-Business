## 7. ADVICE — Automate Growth Experiments Until They Plateau
- Turn repetitive growth work into a loop that generates variants, measures experiments, ships winners, and maintains holdouts.
- Reserve human judgment for finding the next hill after optimization reaches a local maximum.
