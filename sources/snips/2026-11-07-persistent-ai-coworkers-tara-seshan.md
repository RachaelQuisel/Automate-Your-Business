## 1. INSIGHT — Be Prolific And Empirical Over Theoretical
- In fast-changing AI markets, prolific empirical experiments beat long theoretical strategy docs.
- Focus writing on the single sharp hypothesis (the eigenquestion) and run fast tests to learn quickly.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/c5b7393d-4b0f-4635-a1da-9e6f6b6c33b4

## 2. INSIGHT — Work Will Shift From Rowing To Steering
- Knowledge work will shift from rowing to steering where agents do execution and people set direction and opinion.
- Steering rises to higher abstractions: choose goals, give feedback, and imagine product futures you want to create.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/c484e0c8-4554-4bd1-8418-bed3443d6659

## 3. INSIGHT — Agents As Persistent Coworkers
- Agents will become persistent coworkers that you interact with like teammates, syncing at cadences and sharing in-progress work.
- Multi-agent multiplayer work requires interfaces for collaborative agent ownership and shared artifacts.

## 4. ADVICE — Elevate Team Ambition With AI Possibilities
- Elevate others' ambitions by reminding teams what's now possible with AI and push for faster, bolder scopes.
- PMs should ask: shouldn't this be bigger, faster, or at a higher scale given new tooling?

## 5. ADVICE — Build For Models Two To Three Months Out
- Build product surfaces for where models will be in two to three months, not for today or a year from now.
- Tie product constructs to model capability and design to get out of the model's way so upgrades remain compatible.

https://share.snipd.com/snip/7f779a35-b369-4707-82ca-48da0e570601
