### Workshop pick: Snip 1, the personal-agent benchmark on nine real tasks
The richest end-to-end workflow in today's file is Claire Vo's benchmark method, because every small business owner now faces the same choice she faced, four capable personal agents and no honest way to choose, and her answer was not a review, it was a test she designed. The loop is complete and teachable with no code. Pick the nine jobs you would actually give an agent, from your own week. Write each one exactly as you would ask it, with the real files and the real mess. Decide what a good result looks like before anyone runs, in a short checklist a scorer can follow. Run every candidate agent on the same nine jobs, score them with a scorer the agent cannot edit, and read the failures, not just the totals. Then assign work and life separately: the agent that wins your inbox may lose your family newspaper, and the scoreboard, kept and re-run when a new agent arrives, is the asset. The workshop is called Test Your Agent on Your Own Nine Jobs. It carries today's other numbers with it: the 53.9 percent ceiling on business automation (Snip 2), the one in ten pass rate on real professional work (Snip 3), and the scorer the agent cannot edit, from the tax rubric and the production change checklist (Snips 3 and 4).
